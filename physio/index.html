FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. You can book directly. Some insurance plans ask for a referral, so check your policy first.

What should I wear to my first visit?

Comfortable clothes that let us see and move the area being treated, such as shorts for knee problems.

How many sessions will I need?

Most people need between 4 and 8 sessions. We give you an honest estimate after your first assessment.

Do you bill my insurance directly?

Yes, we bill most major insurers directly, so you only pay any part your plan does not cover.
