About

Ten years, forty countries, one camera bag.

I photograph places that ask you to slow down. Most of my work begins before sunrise, often after a long hike, waiting for a few minutes of perfect light.

I work with travel brands, magazines and private collectors, and I run small workshops for people who want to see the landscape differently.
