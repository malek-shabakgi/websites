Good to know.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, when a chair is free. Booking online guarantees your time, especially on Fridays and Saturdays.

How early should I arrive?

Five minutes is perfect. If you are more than 10 minutes late we may need to shorten or move your appointment.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Free cancellation up to 12 hours before. Use the link in your confirmation email.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes, from age 4. The Father & Son package is our most popular weekend booking.
